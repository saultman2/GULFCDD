## Indexing and sitemap handoff

The public site must be published before indexing can be requested. Once the current build is live, the owner or verified site administrator should complete this one-time distribution pass in the search tools they control:

1. In Google Search Console, inspect and request indexing for the exact canonical URL: `https://www.gulfcdd.com/blog/gulf-coast-digital-marketing-seo-geo-guide/`.
2. In the same Search Console property, submit or confirm the sitemap: `https://www.gulfcdd.com/sitemap.xml`. Confirm that it was read successfully rather than submitting duplicate sitemap URLs.
3. If GulfCDD has a verified Bing Webmaster Tools property, submit the same sitemap there and use its URL submission feature only for this new canonical URL.
4. After publishing, verify the guide returns HTTP 200, has one trailing-slash canonical matching the URL above, and appears in the live sitemap. Verify `/robots.txt` still advertises that sitemap.

Search-console ownership, indexing requests, and partner communications are intentionally not automated in this repository. Do not place credentials in the site or send outreach until the recipient, relationship, and final copy are approved. An indexing request is a crawl request, not a guarantee of indexing or ranking.

## Approval rules

- Do not buy links or use bulk submission services.
- Do not ask for a link before establishing a legitimate relationship.
- Do not promise reciprocal links unless both links are genuinely useful.
- Mark paid sponsorship links as sponsored where applicable.
- Use brand or natural descriptive anchors; do not force exact-match keywords.
- Keep claims, client results, awards, and credentials accurate and verifiable.
- Send no outreach until the recipient, relationship, and final copy are approved.

## Tracking fields

Track each prospect with: organization, URL, contact, relationship, relevant page, pitch angle, target GulfCDD URL, outreach date, follow-up date, response, link URL, link type, referral traffic, and next action.
